Where plumbing margin hides

Three questions most plumbing books can't answer.

Service vs new construction

Service calls bring higher rates and fast cash. New construction brings volume, draws, and long waits to get paid. Lumped together on one P&L, you can't tell which side carries the shop.

Water heater installs

Your bread-and-butter job. But the unit, fittings, permit, haul-away, and a second tech's time add up. We cost each install so you know your real margin and price it with confidence.

Callbacks and warranty work

A callback is a truck roll you don't get paid for. When warranty visits get tracked back to the original job, you see which work types and which installs keep coming back.
