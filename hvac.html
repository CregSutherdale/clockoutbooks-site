Where HVAC margin hides

Three questions most HVAC books can't answer.

Do your maintenance agreements pay?

Agreement revenue comes in up front. The tune-up truck rolls come later. Without job costing, you can't tell whether the plan pays for the visits, or just feels like it does.

Replacement vs repair margins

A system replacement looks huge on the invoice. Equipment cost, extra labor hours, and callbacks can leave it thinner than a good repair day. We break margin out by job type so you know where to push.

Seasonal cash swings

Summer and winter peaks, then the shoulder months. Closed books every month show what you need in the bank to carry techs through the slow weeks.
