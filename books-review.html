What happens on the call

Twenty minutes, start to finish.

  1. Minutes 0 to 3

    Quick hello. How many techs, what software you run, and the one money headache that's bugging you most.

  2. Minutes 3 to 15

    You share your screen. We walk through the books together: whether bank and card accounts are reconciled, what's sitting uncategorized, deposits that never landed, who owes you money, whether jobs are tracked, and whether your field software is double-posting.

  3. Minutes 15 to 20

    Straight talk. What's broken, what it's likely costing you, and a cleanup price on the spot. Every call ends with a price. You decide later, or never.

What to have open

  • QuickBooks Online (or Desktop, or whatever you use), logged in
  • Your field software too, if you have it: ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge
  • A computer, not a phone, so the screen is readable
  • Whoever actually does the books. Bring them. They'll know where things are.

What you'll hear

  • What's broken, in plain words
  • What it's likely costing you: job numbers you can't trust, cash surprises, a close that never happens
  • What we'd fix first
  • A cleanup price, before we hang up

What we never ask for

  • Passwords or logins
  • File uploads or exports
  • Bank or card numbers

You keep control of the screen the whole time. We don't record the call unless you say yes first. See our privacy notice.

What the review is, and isn't

It's a fast, honest look at the books by someone who only works with trade shops: HVAC, plumbing, and electrical. It's not free bookkeeping. We don't fix anything on the call and we don't take work home. If the books are in good shape, we'll tell you that too.

If you hire us for a Cleanup, it ends with a Job Profit Report: margin by job type for your shop, built on books you can finally trust. On Monthly Books + Job Costing, you get that report every month. See pricing.

Good fit if

  • You run an HVAC, plumbing, or electrical shop with a few techs or more
  • You're tired of doing the books after hours
  • The books are behind, or nobody trusts them
  • Your field software and QuickBooks don't agree

We do bookkeeping and job costing. We're not a CPA firm and we don't do taxes. Your tax preparer keeps doing that.
